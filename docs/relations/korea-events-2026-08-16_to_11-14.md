# Korean Peninsula event list, 16 Aug to 14 Nov 2026

Compiled 15 Nov 2026 for testing a GDELT-based North Korea relations/tone measure (partners KOR, USA, JPN, CHN, RUS).

## Method and limits (read first)

- Step 1: Watchfloor API `GET /zone?entity=03_Korea_Peninsula&day=` read for every built day 28 Sep to 13 Nov 2026 (47 requests, 2 s apart, no POSTs). Stories were used to spot candidates only. Days 18 Oct (16 stories), 10 Oct (5), 11 Oct (2) and 6-9 Nov (4-19 stories each) were thin; this looks like low coverage in the store, not a quiet peninsula.
- Step 2: web search (extended mode). Search returns summaries; I did not open each article. Publication dates come from the URL path or the result snippet; "pub n/d" means the date was not visible. Where a date is in the URL I give it.
- Dates are UTC. Korean launches reported at KST morning/midday fall on the same UTC date; KST early-morning events fall on the previous UTC day only where noted. KST = UTC+9, JST = UTC+9.
- Confidence: "2+" = two or more independent outlets in search results; "single" = one outlet or one wire syndicated; "unverified" = conflicting or only aggregator/low-grade. Syndicated AP/Reuters copies count once.
- Launch counts conflict between outlets (UPI: 3 Oct was 15th ballistic event of 2026; Yonhap-based: 29 Oct was 17th; Aug 12 was 11th, 12 Sep 13th per USNI). Not reconciled.
- Several results looked like low-quality aggregators or tabloids (Daily Star, NaturalNews/NewsTarget, Peepals, kayhan.ir). They are excluded or flagged.
- Context just before the window (not counted): 15 Aug Lee Jae Myung Liberation Day proposal for talks to formally end the Korean War (Korea Times, https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/southkorea/politics/20260815/lee-proposes-talks-with-nk-to-formally-end-korean-war-halt-pyongyangs-nuclear-program); 12 Aug SRBM from Wonsan, 700+ km (Asia Business Daily, https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026081208422323587); 14 Aug NK warning over US nuclear strategy (Korea Times, https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/foreignaffairs/northkorea/20260814/n-korea-warns-of-bolstering-nuclear-deterrent-over-us-new-nuclear-strategy).
